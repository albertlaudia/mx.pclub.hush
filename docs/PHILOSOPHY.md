# Philosophy — `hush.`

> Why this name. Why this product. Why no streak, no flame, no score, no social. The whole thing in one document.

## The name, in one breath

**`hush.`** is a state, not an action.

To *lock* is to coerce — to prevent an opening, to force a closed state, to deny access. The product doesn't coerce.

To *hush* is to settle — to make quiet, to invite attention, to release noise. A hush has a beginning and an end. A hush is shared (a hush falls over a crowd) but also deeply personal (you hush alone with a verse). A hush is universal — every language has a word for it, every faith practices it, every person needs it.

The name is the *verb made noun* — the action frozen into a place. You don't do a hush. You *enter* one. The product is that place.

## The seed

The original seed for this product was a single line: *"Be still, and know that I am God."* (Psalm 46:10).

The Hebrew behind "be still" is **רפּוּ** (*rapu*) — *let go, release, make room*. Not silence. Release. The verse is not "shut up" — it's "stop striving". Stop holding. Let go of the noise long enough to notice what was always there.

"Hush" carries that. To hush is to release the noise, to make room, to attend.

The product is the hush. The verse is what you notice in it.

## The five things `hush.` means

### 1. Invitation, not command

A lock is a command. *"Lock your phone. Lock in. Lock down."* The user is being told what to do.

A hush is an invitation. *"Hush for a moment. Let it land. Then continue."* The user is being asked to do something they already want to do.

The product never commands. It never nags. It never guilt-trips. It doesn't say *"you missed 3 days"*. It doesn't say *"don't break the chain"*. It offers the practice. The user accepts or doesn't. The product doesn't take it personally.

### 2. State, not task

A lock is a thing you do to a thing. *"Lock the door."* The lock persists. The action is binary — locked or unlocked.

A hush is a place you enter. *"A hush fell over the room."* The hush is temporary. The state is finite. You are in it, then you are not.

The product is a place, not a task. The user enters the practice, the practice ends, the user returns to their day. The product doesn't *track* the practice as a metric — it *holds* it as a moment.

### 3. Quiet, not loud

A lock is mechanical. It clicks, it clamps, it forces. It's a hard sound.

A hush is soft. The word ends in /ʃ/ — a fricative that trails off into breath. The word itself is a kind of hush.

The product follows its name. No streak. No flame. No emoji. No exclamation marks. No *"extraordinary!"*. No *"share with friends!"*. No *"amazing prayer journey!"*. The voice is lowercase, sentence case, the period at the end. The colors are deep teal and warm cream — the colors of a leather-bound book, not a workout tracker. The amber dot is the wax seal, not the flame.

### 4. Private, not public

A lock is often about others. You lock the door against intruders. You lock the phone from curious eyes. The lock is a boundary with the world.

A hush is between you and the moment. A hush can be observed (a crowd goes quiet) but it's not *performed* for the crowd. The hush is internal.

The product is private. No public profile. No "share my streak". No leaderboard. No Section. No social graph. The user's practice is between the user and the verse. The data lives on the device. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. The brand is a journal, not a stage.

### 5. Universal, not specific

A lock is specific. You lock *this* door, not *that* one. The object of the lock is named.

A hush is universal. Every language has a word for it. Every faith has a practice of it. The Muslim calls it *sukun* in salah. The Jew calls it *shetika* in davening. The Buddhist calls it *samatha* in vipassana. The Christian calls it *hésychia* in contemplative prayer. The secular meditator calls it silence.

The product is multi-faith by design. The verses are chosen for their universality, not their sect-specificity. The brand has no cross, no rosary, no lotus, no crescent. Just the wordmark, the dot, the verse, the practice.

## What the name rejects

The name `hush.` is a *refusal* of several things:

| What `hush.` is not | Why |
|---|---|
| **Not "lock"** | Locking is coercion. Hushing is invitation. |
| **Not "streak"** | A streak measures. A hush holds. |
| **Not "pray"** | Prayer is one form of practice. Hush is the universal form. |
| **Not "devotion"** | Devotion is a posture. Hush is a state. |
| **Not "ritual"** | Ritual is a sequence. Hush is a moment. |
| **Not "discipline"** | Discipline is a habit. Hush is a pause. |
| **Not "practice"** | (Well, it is practice. But the *product* is the hush. The *activity* is the practice. The *state* is the hush.) |

## What the name permits

`hush.` is a name that has opinions. Those opinions shape the product:

| Permitted | Why |
|---|---|
| A daily reminder (in v0.2) | A friend whispering "the hush is ready" — not a notification screaming "YOU MISSED A DAY". |
| A home widget (in v0.2) | A quiet glance at the mark — not a counter, not a flame, just the dot. |
| Settings, but minimal | Three rows: window, today, reset. Anything more is noise. |
| One light haptic on "continue" | The haptic is the *seal* — the moment closed. Not a reward, not a punishment. |
| A verse for today | Not "your 47th day". Not "your longest streak". Today is today. |
| A name on the about page | "made by pclub". That's it. No founder story. No manifesto. |

## What the name demands

If the product is called `hush.`, the product has to *be* a hush. That means:

- **No buzzes.** Notifications are opt-in, infrequent, and quiet.
- **No nudges.** The product does not say "are you sure you want to skip?".
- **No metrics on the surface.** The streak, the count, the "best ever" are gone or hidden.
- **No animation noise.** No bouncing, no spinning, no celebratory confetti. Fade-ins, gentle transitions, hush.
- **No copy that shouts.** No "AMAZING", no "EXTRAORDINARY", no "INCREDIBLE". The voice is the same voice you'd use in a library.
- **No "you".** The product says "begin", not "you should begin". It says "see you tomorrow", not "we'll see you tomorrow". The user is alone in the hush.

## The wordmark as philosophy

The visual mark is the philosophy in shapes.

`hush.` in DM Serif Display:
- The two `h`s are tall, vertical, like columns or pillars. They frame.
- The `u` is a low curve, a bowl, a vessel.
- The `s` is a serpentine, a meander, a thing that flows.
- The period is the end. The hush is finite.

The amber dot is above the `u` — centered, framed by the two `h`s, like a single drop held in a vessel. The dot is the *one thing* the user notices. The dot is the small mark of attention. The dot is the wax seal on the hush.

The deep teal is the color of a leather-bound book on a wooden desk. The cream is the page. The amber is the candle in the corner. The product is a place you go, not a thing you do.

## The word, the brand, the product, the user

`hush.` is the word.
The brand is the wordmark, the dot, the colors, the voice.
The product is the three screens: onboarding, home, practice.
The user is a whole person, not a metric.

The word shapes the brand. The brand shapes the product. The product shapes the user. The user is in the hush.

## What "hush" sounds like

The word is soft. /hʌʃ/.
- The /h/ is breath.
- The /ʌ/ is a relaxed vowel — the schwa's cousin.
- The /ʃ/ trails off into nothing.

The word doesn't punch. It exhales. The word is itself a kind of hush.

## What "hush" is not

A whisper. A whisper is secretive. A hush is shared.
A silence. A silence is the absence of sound. A hush is the *presence* of quiet — it has texture, it has weight, it has duration.
A command. A command is given by someone with authority. A hush can be self-given.
A meditation. A meditation is a specific practice. A hush is what *underlies* every practice.
A pause. A pause is an interruption. A hush is the space the pause makes room for.

## The final word

`hush.` is the answer to: *what do you call a product that is not a blocker, not a counter, not a streak, not a flame, not a community, not a subscription, not a notification?*

You call it `hush.`

A brief, quiet moment. The user enters. The verse is there. The user attends. The user continues. The product says: *"see you tomorrow."*

The hush is the product. The product is the hush. The user is in it.
